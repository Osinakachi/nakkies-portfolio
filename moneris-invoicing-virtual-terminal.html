Context and problem

Not every payment happens at a counter. Merchants bill customers after a service, take card details over the phone, and need to know whether an invoice was paid. These are complex payment flows with real money and sensitive card data, and merchants often do them infrequently, so they forget the steps between uses.

Before the redesign, P66 sample invoices were created outside the portal and payment entry used one dense form.

Constraints

  • Card data must be validated before it is submitted, and errors must be specific
  • Sensitive data stays masked wherever it is shown
  • Infrequent users who need the flow to explain itself
  • P67 sample PCI rules limited what could be stored or shown on screen

Key decisions

Validate while the merchant types

What I chose
The form tells the merchant when card details pass validation and are ready to process, and explains problems in plain words.
Why
Catching mistakes before submit avoids declined payments and re-entry.
