Part 1: Sign-in and enrollment

Sign-in had to be secure without frustrating merchants who are not technical. Enrollment had two audiences: people new to Moneris and people who already had an account.

A focused login

What I chose
One clear primary action, with Forgot your password and Create an account visible without hunting.
Why
People arrive stressed when they are locked out.
What it cost
Less room for announcements.

A verification code with Remember my device

What I chose
Merchants confirm with a code, and can choose to remember the device.
Why
P47 sample it balances security with convenience for merchants who sign in daily
What it cost
P48 sample the security team and the product manager

A new vs existing customer diverter on mobile

What I chose
The mobile journey splits at the start.
Why
People should not fill in a form meant for someone else.
What it cost
One extra choice up front.

Errors that say how to fix the problem

What I chose
P49 sample a specific message plus the next step to fix it
Why
Vague errors create support calls.
What it cost
More copy to write and maintain.

Part 2: The design system

No shared system existed, so each page reinvented buttons, tables and filters. I built the system as I redesigned screens, so every component was proven in a real flow before it was documented.

  • Components with documented states: default, hover, selected and focused
  • A shared header, navigation and layout structure used by every page
  • Table, filter, date range and modal patterns that came out of the reports work
  • Accessibility rules built into each component rather than added later

How I got developers to adopt it: P51 sample workshops, written documentation and build reviews
