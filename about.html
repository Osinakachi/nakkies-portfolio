About

Senior product designer with 8+ years of experience, turning complex payments workflows into simple, accessible products that merchants trust.

I design software for people who have to get things right under pressure. At Moneris, I work on the Go Portal, the place merchants of every size go to see their sales, take payments, resolve problems and manage their accounts.

I started in graphic design and moved into UX and product design, which is why I care about both how something looks and whether it works. My process is research first, then design in the open: I test with real merchants, document the trade-offs, and build shared components so each screen is faster and more consistent than the last.

I hold a Master of Digital Experience Innovation from the University of Waterloo. I live in Canada and work with teams across product, analytics and engineering.

What I do

  • Product design

    End-to-end design of complex payments and reporting workflows, from first sketch to developer handoff.

  • UX research

    Usability testing on usertesting.com, interviews with clients, support-ticket analysis and journey mapping.

  • Design systems

    Component libraries with documented states, built into real screens so teams adopt them.

  • Accessibility

    Designing to WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard order, labels and errors that do not rely on colour.

  • Prototyping and handoff

    Interactive prototypes, specs and build reviews so what ships matches what was designed.

  • Fintech and payments

    Invoicing, virtual terminals, recurring billing, stored cards, installments and fraud tools.
