Context and problem

Stored cards, repeat billing, currency conversion and installment plans are powerful, and easy to get wrong. A setting that applies to every device in a store can change what hundreds of customers see at checkout. Merchants needed to understand what each option does before they turn it on.

P86 sample settings were buried, jargon-heavy and gave no sense of impact

Constraints

  • Settings can apply across every device that supports a feature in a store
  • Merchants using Moneris Checkout configure installments from a control panel
  • Stored card data must stay protected
  • P87 sample card brand rules decide which plan lengths can be offered

Key decisions

Say what a setting does in the setting

What I chose
The installments panel explains in plain language that merchants choose which monthly plan lengths to display, and activates each choice with a checkbox.
Why
Merchants decide with the consequence in front of them.
What it cost
More copy on screen.

Protect against losing changes

What I chose
Leaving with unsaved edits triggers a confirmation that names the scope, all devices that support installments across the store.
Why
A change here touches every device, so a silent loss or a silent save would both be costly.
What it cost
One extra step when leaving.

Clear enabled and disabled states

What I chose
The feature has a distinct, readable disabled state.
Why
Merchants must always know whether customers can see installments.
What it cost
Two states per setting to maintain.

P92 sample Show saved cards without exposing them

What I chose
P92 sample Cards in Vault show only the brand and last four digits.
Why
P92 sample Staff can find a customer’s card without ever seeing the full number.
What it cost
P92 sample Edge cases need a support step because full numbers cannot be copied.
